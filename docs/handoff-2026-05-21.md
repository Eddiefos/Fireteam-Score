## Track 1 — Interactive course map

### Problem
The map on CourseDetailScreen was locked (`interactive={false}`), zoomed too far in (level 14), and had no pin — making it impossible to know what you were looking at.

### What changed

**`src/screens/CourseDetailScreen.jsx`**
- Replaced `MapGL` lazy import with a `CourseMap` wrapper component that includes `Marker` in the same lazy chunk (no extra bundle cost)
- Removed `interactive={false}` — drag-pan and pinch-zoom now work on touch
- Zoom `14` → `13` — shows surrounding area/forest instead of street-block level
- Added an orange (`FT.orange`) circular marker with white border at the course coordinates
- Skipped `<NavigationControl>` — redundant on mobile (users pinch to zoom)
